# Third party component

Screenguard's macOS installer includes `displayplacer` v1.4.0 from
<https://github.com/jakehilborn/displayplacer>, licensed under the MIT License.
The build helper downloads the upstream architecture-specific release and checks
its pinned SHA-256 before packaging it. The complete upstream MIT license text
is included beside this notice in `LICENSE`.

Screenguard also includes code from `ddc-macos` v0.2.2 and `ddc` v0.2.2,
both licensed under MIT. Their license texts are included as `LICENSE-ddc-macos`
and `LICENSE-ddc`.

Screenguard directly uses `core-foundation` v0.10.1 under either the MIT or
Apache-2.0 license. Both license texts are included beside this notice.
